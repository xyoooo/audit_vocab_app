The company reported higher net sales during the year, supported by strong demand in several product categories. Gross margin improved because cost of sales grew more slowly than revenue.

Operating income increased even though operating expenses included higher research and development spending. Management noted that liquidity remained strong because operating cash flow exceeded capital expenditures, resulting in positive free cash flow.

The notes to the financial statements discuss accounts receivable, inventory, deferred revenue, and revenue recognition. The company also evaluates goodwill and other long-lived assets for impairment when events indicate that carrying amounts may not be recoverable.

Management assessed internal control over financial reporting and did not identify a material weakness. The report also discusses foreign currency exposure, interest rate risk, credit risk, fair value measurements, derivatives, and contingencies.
